Pick a production config. Three sentences defending it, including latency.
hybrid alpha 0.6, top 5, no reranker.
Production is hybrid search (0.6 dense + 0.4 BM25) returning the top 5, with no reranker. 
It scores recall@5 1.000 and nDCG@5 0.998 on the 30 tuning questions and 1.000 on my 5 held-out questions, at 3.5 ms p50. 
The best grid run with a reranker scored slightly lower (0.996) and took 334 ms, about 100 times slower for no gain,
so the reranker isn’t worth its cost on this small knowledge base.
