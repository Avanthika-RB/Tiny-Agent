# Password and Account Security

## Password rules

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and contain three of the four categories: uppercase, lowercase, digits, symbols. Passwords expire every 180 days. You will receive reminder emails at 14, 7, and 1 day before expiry. A password cannot match any of your previous 12 passwords. Passphrases such as four unrelated words are allowed and encouraged because they are easier to remember and harder to guess than short complex strings.

## Changing your password

### On Windows

1. Press Ctrl+Alt+Delete.
2. Choose Change a password.
3. Enter the old password, then the new password twice.
4. Lock and unlock the laptop once so cached credentials refresh. If you skip this step, Outlook and Teams will keep prompting with the old password for up to an hour.

### On macOS

1. Open System Settings, then Users and Groups.
2. Click Change Password next to your account.
3. Enter the old and new passwords.
4. Open Keychain Access and update the saved entries for Outlook and the VPN client, otherwise they will keep failing silently.

## Locked account

An account locks after five failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes. The lock clears automatically after 30 minutes. If you need access sooner, go to the self-service password portal, choose Unlock account, and enter the one-time code sent to your registered phone. If your registered phone is not available, the helpdesk can unlock your account after verifying your identity with your employee id and manager's name.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is mandatory for VPN, email, and all cloud applications. The authenticator app is the preferred method. SMS codes are available as a fallback but are slower and less secure. If you change phones, re-register the authenticator app before you wipe the old device. If you have already wiped it, the helpdesk must reset your MFA, which requires a video call for identity verification and takes about 20 minutes.

## Phishing

Never enter your password on a page you reached from an email link. The real sign-in page always shows the company logo and the address ends in example.com. Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button in Outlook. Do not forward them. If you did enter your password on a suspicious page, change it immediately using the steps above and inform the security team the same day.
