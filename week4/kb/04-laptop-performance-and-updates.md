# Laptop Performance and Updates

## Update policy

Operating system updates are pushed on the second Tuesday of each month. You will see a notification with a seven-day window to restart at a convenient time. After seven days the laptop restarts automatically outside working hours. Security patches rated critical may be pushed outside this schedule with a 24-hour window.

## Slow after an update

After a major update the laptop rebuilds its search index and scans files for the security agent. This takes between 30 and 90 minutes depending on how much data is on the disk, and the laptop will feel sluggish during that period. The fix is patience plus two restarts: restart once immediately after the update finishes, then again an hour later. If the laptop is still slow the following day, file a ticket and include a screenshot of Task Manager or Activity Monitor sorted by CPU.

## General slowness

Before filing a ticket, check these in order:

1. How many browser tabs are open. More than 40 tabs is the most common cause of slowness we see.
2. Whether the disk is more than 90 percent full. Empty the Downloads folder and the Recycle Bin or Trash.
3. How long since the last full restart. Sleeping the laptop for two weeks is not a restart. Shut it down fully once a week.
4. Whether a backup or sync is running. The cloud drive client shows a progress indicator in the system tray or menu bar.

If none of those apply and the laptop is more than three years old, it may be eligible for replacement. Ask your manager to raise a hardware refresh request.

## Battery

Batteries are consumables and are expected to lose about 20 percent of capacity after two years. A laptop that no longer lasts a two-hour meeting on battery can have the battery replaced under a low-priority ticket. Keep the laptop plugged in when at a desk, but let it run down to below 20 percent at least once a month to keep the battery gauge calibrated.

## Fans running constantly

Usually the security agent or a stuck browser tab. Restart first. If the fans run loud at idle for more than a day after a restart, file a ticket; the fan may be clogged with dust and Facilities can clean it.
